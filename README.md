# Behavioral Interview Preparation

A workspace for collecting behavioral questions, developing stories from my own experience, and practicing clear, specific answers.

## Workflow

1. Collect a question and identify the skill it tests.
2. Choose a real experience and draft it using the story template below.
3. Make my contribution, reasoning, and outcome explicit. Use numbers only when I can support them.
4. Practice a concise spoken answer, then prepare for follow-up questions.
5. Record feedback and revise the story.

## Story template

- **Title:**
- **Questions this story could answer:**
- **Situation:** What was happening? Include only the context needed to understand the problem.
- **Task:** What was I responsible for? What constraints or stakes mattered?
- **Action:** What did I personally do, why did I choose that approach, and how did I work with others?
- **Result:** What changed? Include evidence, and distinguish team results from my contribution.
- **Reflection:** What did I learn, and what would I do differently?
- **Short spoken version:** Aim for about two minutes, leaving room for follow-ups.
- **Likely follow-ups:** Alternatives, tradeoffs, disagreements, evidence, and lessons.
- **Practice notes:** Date, feedback, and next revision.

Use anonymized details where needed; keep confidential information out of the repository.

## Starter questions

- Tell me about yourself and the experiences most relevant to this role.
- Tell me about a challenging problem you solved.
- Describe a time you took ownership beyond your assigned responsibilities.
- Tell me about a disagreement with a teammate or stakeholder. How did you resolve it?
- Describe a time you influenced a decision without formal authority.
- Tell me about a mistake or failure and what you changed afterward.
- Describe a time you received difficult feedback.
- Tell me about a time you worked through ambiguity.
- Describe how you handled competing priorities or a tight deadline.
- Tell me about a tradeoff between speed and quality.
- Describe a time you helped someone else succeed.
- Tell me about a measurable improvement you delivered for users or your team.
